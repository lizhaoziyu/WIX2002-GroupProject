# Product Backlog: User Stories

**Project**: EcoSwap (WIX2002 Group Project)  
**Author**: WANG ZIMING (Product Owner), Week 5  
**Scope**: Four core modules, ten initial user stories.

---

## Module 1: Post an Item
1. **As a** student who is moving out, **I want to** post items I no longer need, **so that** other students can reuse them instead of throwing them away.
2. **As a** student, **I want to** upload photos and descriptions of my items, **so that** other students can clearly understand what I am offering.
3. **As a** student, **I want to** select the category and condition of my items, **so that** other students can easily find suitable items.

## Module 2: Browse and Filter
4. **As a** student, **I want to** browse available items by category, **so that** I can quickly find things I need.
5. **As a** student, **I want to** search for items using keywords, **so that** I can find specific items without scrolling through every listing.
6. **As a** student on a limited budget, **I want to** filter items by condition, **so that** I can choose items that meet my needs.

## Module 3: Item Detail and Request
7. **As a** student interested in an item, **I want to** view its photos, description, and condition, **so that** I can decide whether it is suitable for me.
8. **As a** student, **I want to** send an exchange request directly from the item details page, **so that** I can easily contact the owner and request the item.

## Module 4: My Records
9. **As a** student who has posted items, **I want to** view and manage all my listings in one place, **so that** I can keep track of the items I am offering.
10. **As a** student participating in item exchanges, **I want to** check the status of my requests, such as pending, accepted, or completed, **so that** I can track the progress of each exchange.

---

> **Note on Actual vs. Planned Scope**: Our final product supports three listing types (`Swap`, `Giveaway`, `Upcycling`) with no selling or payment. The product also shipped additional features outside this initial backlog (such as Admin Dashboard, In-App Chat, Pickup Map, Impact Page, Reports & Support Tickets), which will be documented in the **Actual Product Backlog** section of the final report.
